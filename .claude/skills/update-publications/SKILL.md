# Update the PEPkit publications list

Follow `automation/update-publications.md` completely, including writing the
review summary to `/tmp/pr-body.md`.

Do not create a branch, commit, push, or open a pull request. In the monthly
workflow a hook does that when you finish. In a local session, leave the
changes for the person to review.
